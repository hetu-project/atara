# Frequently asked questions

**Do I have to start as a Light worker?**
Yes. Every node enters at the first level. Collateral and compute can qualify you for a higher level on two of the five requirements, but reputation and track record start at zero for every node and are required for promotion.

**Can I buy a Chain worker or Adjudicator license?**
No. Licenses are not transferable. They are earned by the node that holds them and lost when its requirements fall below the line.

**What return should I expect?**
This documentation does not quote yields. Income comes only from real settlement volume; there is no reward for posting collateral. The structure is on [A worked example](../earnings/worked-example.md).

**Does the network pay interest on my collateral?**
No, and it never will. You may hold part of your collateral in whitelisted tokenised sovereign debt; the yield on that is your own income and does not pass through the network. See [Collateral](../collateral/two-layers.md).

**What happens to my collateral if another node errs?**
Nothing. Penalties are charged to the node that erred and go to the Reserve. No node's collateral covers another node's error.

**Can I run more than one node?**
Yes. Each is a separate node with its own reputation starting at zero. For concentration caps, affiliated nodes are aggregated.

**Can I both run a node and be a clearing member?**
Yes, through separate entities with separate books. See [Institutional roles](../institutions/institutional-roles.md).

**What if I stop operating for a while?**
Reputation decays by about 2 percent per month and track record is measured on a rolling window. A node that stops working can fall below the line for its level and be demoted.

**How do I leave?**
Apply to exit. Collateral is released after a cooling period. Exit does not require anyone's approval.

**Who decides disputes about my reputation score?**
No one. Every event that moves the score is signed and ordered; anyone can replay the events and recompute it.
