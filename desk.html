Why this is hard

Large OTC runs on relationships, because the alternative is unverifiable risk.

A large trade with a stranger opens three questions at once. Whether the USDT is clean. Whether the other side will actually send. Whether the counterparty is who they say they are. Most desks answer all three the same way: only trade inside a network of introductions, and meet in person for anything large. We answer them with a process instead.

01

We collect the documents, so neither side hands papers to a stranger.

Identity, corporate registration, source of funds, wallet provenance, sanctions screening. Both sides submit to us, and we tell each side what cleared. Nobody sends a passport scan to a counterparty they have never met.

02

Matched from a book we already hold, not from introductions.

We carry both sides. Fiat holders looking for USDT, and USDT holders looking for fiat. A request does not go out to a network and wait for someone to vouch for you. It gets matched against size and side that are already verified and on the book.

Live today
03

The deal closes without anyone getting on a plane.

A traditional large block ends with two people in a room, one carrying cash, the other transferring on the spot. That is the reason these trades take as long as they do. Ours completes remotely, because custody and verification replace the need to be standing there.

What we verify

Checked before a trade is matched, not after.

Screening after the fact is worthless. By the time tainted USDT is in your wallet, the exposure already exists. Everything below happens before either side is told a counterparty is available.

We turn trades down

A wallet that fails provenance screening does not get matched, and the trade does not proceed. This is the part of the service that costs us revenue, and it is the reason the other part is worth anything.

Questions

The things counterparties ask first.

Do you take custody of the fiat?
No. The fiat moves bank to bank between the two counterparties, directly. We are never in that path. Our custody is over the USDT leg only, which is where the settlement risk and the provenance risk actually sit.
What stops the fiat side from taking the USDT and not paying?
The USDT is with us, not with them. It is released only after the USDT side confirms the fiat has landed in their account. Until that confirmation, the fiat side has no claim on it and no way to move it.
What stops the USDT side from losing their USDT?
They transfer to our custody, not to the counterparty. If the fiat never arrives, the trade does not complete and the USDT returns to them. Their exposure is to us for the duration of the trade, not to a counterparty they have never met.
How do I know the USDT I receive is clean?
Two screens. The wallet is screened before the counterparty reaches the book, and the specific incoming transaction is screened again when it arrives in custody. A transaction that fails the second screen is not released to you, and the trade is unwound.
Are you licensed?
We operate through licensed channel partners rather than holding a licence ourselves. If the specific regulatory standing matters for your trade, ask us and we will tell you exactly which entity and which permission covers it, in writing, before you commit.
What size do you work with, and what does it cost?
Quoted per trade against size, pair and the corridor. We do not publish a rate card, because a real quote depends on which banks are on each end. Send the size and side and you will get a number, not a range.
How long does a trade take?
Verification is the long pole, and it depends entirely on how fast documents come back from both sides. Once both sides are verified and on the book, matching and settlement are not the bottleneck. We will give you a timeline for your specific trade before you start, not an average.

Send us a size and a side.

You will get a specific answer: whether we have the other side, what documents we need, and what it will cost. If we cannot do your trade we will say so in the first reply.
