# Anti-concentration rules

Three rules keep any single operator from owning the network's judgment or its ledger. All three are enforced by code.

## Licenses are not transferable

A license cannot be bought, sold, transferred or inherited. It attaches to the node that earned it, and it is lost when that node's requirements fall below the line. A large holder can fund many Light workers; each one starts at zero reputation and climbs on its own record.

## One category, one role

Within a single asset category, a node may not attest, adjudicate and trade as principal at the same time. The referee does not play. Across categories a node may hold different roles. Category is a tag on the account, and the exclusion is checked by code.

## Concentration caps

* An Adjudicator may handle at most 20 percent of the value under adjudication across the network.
* A Chain worker may record at most 10 percent of ledger volume.

Affiliated entities are aggregated when the cap is measured. Collateral and compute posted above the cap earn no additional quota. Scale does not buy a license; at most it buys the option to fill a quota once the license has been earned.
