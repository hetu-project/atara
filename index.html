Frequently asked questions

Do you compete with payment networks?

No. We don't hold your customer relationship and we don't originate business on your rails. We make the payments that run over them programmable.

How is this different from escrow?

Escrow is a third party holding funds and a human deciding when to release them. Ours is the release logic written into the payment.

Is agent payment available now?

Not yet. It's in preview. Fiat corridors and conditional transfers are live today. Join early access and we'll bring you in when the agent surface opens.

How do I integrate?

Through the SDK or the REST API. Start with the docs, or talk to us if you need a corridor we don't list yet.
